## The Dropshipping P&L (Per Unit)

```
Revenue (Selling Price)                    €XX.00
  − Cost of Goods (Supplier)              −€XX.00
  − Shipping (to customer)                −€XX.00
  − Payment Processing (2.9% + €0.30)     −€XX.00
  − Platform Fee (Shopify/etc, amortised) −€XX.00
  − Estimated Returns (% of units)        −€XX.00
  ─────────────────────────────────────────
  Gross Profit Per Unit                    €XX.00

  − Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)       −€XX.00
  ─────────────────────────────────────────
  Contribution Margin Per Order            €XX.00
```

**Target benchmarks**:
- Gross margin before CAC: 60–75% of selling price
- CAC should not exceed 30% of selling price
- Contribution margin target: >25% of selling price

## Markup Multiple Guide

| Supplier Cost | Minimum Sell Price | Recommended Sell Price |
|---|---|---|
| €2–5 | €12 | €15–20 |
| €5–10 | €22 | €28–40 |
| €10–20 | €35 | €50–70 |
| €20–40 | €70 | €90–120 |

Rule: Never go below 4x markup on physical goods. Target 5–6x for lightweight items.

## CAC Estimation by Platform (2024 benchmarks)

| Platform | Avg CPM | Avg CTR | Avg CVR | Est. CAC |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Facebook/Instagram | €8–20 | 1.5–3% | 1–3% | €15–50 |
| TikTok Ads | €5–12 | 2–5% | 0.8–2% | €12–40 |
| Google Shopping | €0.50–2 CPC | N/A | 2–5% | €20–60 |
| Pinterest | €4–8 | 0.5–1.5% | 0.5–1.5% | €25–70 |

**CAC Formula**:
```
CAC = CPM / 1000 × (1/CTR) × (1/CVR)
Example: €15 CPM, 2% CTR, 2% CVR
CAC = 15/1000 × (1/0.02) × (1/0.02) = €37.50
```

## LTV Estimation

For repeat-purchase categories (consumables, pet supplies, cleaning):
```
LTV = Average Order Value × Purchase Frequency × Avg Customer Lifespan
```

For one-time purchase (most dropship items):
```
LTV ≈ AOV × (1 + Referral Rate)
```
Assume referral rate of 5–15% for high-STEPPS products.

## The 10X Threshold

A product opportunity qualifies as "10X potential" when:
- Bull case ROI on initial test budget ≥ 10x
- Market TAM (Total Addressable Market) supports scaling to €500K+ annual revenue
- Gold Rush window estimated ≥ 3 months remaining
- STEPPS score ≥ 22/30 (organic amplification reduces CAC at scale)

A product is "total loss risk" when:
- Return rate penalty > 25% (fragile, sizing-dependent)
- Competing brands have ≥ 4.5 star average (moat is strong)
- Facebook Ad Library shows 100+ active ads (saturated)
- Google Trends slope has peaked and is declining
