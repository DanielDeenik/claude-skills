# STEPPS Deep Dive — By Product Category

## What STEPPS Predicts
STEPPS doesn't predict sales directly — it predicts **organic amplification**. A high STEPPS
score means the product markets itself through sharing, which lowers CAC and extends the
Gold Rush window before saturation hits.

---

## Category Benchmarks

### Pet Products
- Social Currency: High (people love showing off well-behaved/happy pets)
- Triggers: High (daily feeding/walking routine = constant reminder)
- Emotion: Very high (pet welfare = strong emotional pull)
- Public: Medium (visible on walks, in photos)
- Practical Value: High (solves real recurring problems)
- Stories: Very high (transformation videos: anxious dog → calm dog)
- **Typical STEPPS: 24–28/30. Among strongest categories.**

### Ergonomic / Wellness (posture, back pain, sleep)
- Social Currency: Medium (not glamorous to share, but "I fixed my back pain" is relatable)
- Triggers: Very high (pain is a daily trigger)
- Emotion: High (relief from chronic discomfort)
- Public: Low (private use)
- Practical Value: Very high
- Stories: High (before/after narratives)
- **Typical STEPPS: 20–25/30.**

### Desk / WFH Accessories
- Social Currency: High ("aesthetic setup" culture on TikTok/YouTube)
- Triggers: High (daily work context)
- Emotion: Medium
- Public: High (setup tours, desk photos)
- Practical Value: Medium–High
- Stories: Medium
- **Typical STEPPS: 18–24/30.**

### Cleaning Gadgets ("CleanTok")
- Social Currency: Medium
- Triggers: High (cleaning routine)
- Emotion: Medium (satisfying = dopamine)
- Public: Medium (shared cleaning videos)
- Practical Value: High
- Stories: High (before/after transformation = viral format)
- **Typical STEPPS: 19–24/30.**

### Generic Utility (cable organisers, kitchen tools)
- Social Currency: Low
- Triggers: Medium
- Emotion: Low
- Public: Low
- Practical Value: High
- Stories: Low
- **Typical STEPPS: 10–15/30. Sustainable but not viral. "Boring heatmap" territory.**

---

## Using STEPPS to Write Ad Creative

Match the highest-scoring trigger to your ad format:
- **Emotion dominant**: Lead with the pain/frustration. Show the before state first.
- **Social Currency dominant**: Lead with identity ("smart people use this").
- **Stories dominant**: Use transformation arc. UGC-style video.
- **Practical Value dominant**: Lead with the feature/benefit demonstration. No fluff.
- **Public dominant**: Show the product being used in a visible social context.
